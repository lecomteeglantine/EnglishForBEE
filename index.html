Useful language

Compare, qualify and explain: scientific English values accurate claims rather than dramatic ones.

Try: “The results suggest…” instead of “This proves…”.

SPEAKING LAB

Give your research a voice

Practise the English of seminars, field reports and scientific discussion.

60-second field update

Your survey found fewer pollinators in Site B. Explain the finding without claiming causation.

Structure: observation → possible explanation → limitation → next step.

Discuss the evidence

A habitat restoration project reports a 15% increase in plant richness after one year. Is it a success?

Language: “One limitation is…” / “We would need to know whether…”

Mini conference

Present a species, an interaction or a method to a non-specialist audience in two minutes.

Checklist: define key terms, give an example, explain why it matters, finish clearly.
